# Chapter Polish Protocol

Read this reference for every chapter-level use of `$reader-facing-polish`. The map and checks are editorial working material; do not paste them into reader-facing prose.

## 1. Scope Gate

Confirm before writing:

- the named chapter and target candidate path;
- the Human authorization for this polishing pass;
- the explicitly accepted `OptimizedDocs/` baseline and primary review record;
- whether separate external research or formal Evidence work has already been completed and routed into that baseline;
- whether unresolved logic or case-boundary issues would make derivation premature.

Run this gate before loading all chapter sources. If the accepted baseline or primary review cannot be identified, or a core Claim／object boundary remains on hold, do not create or revise the canonical `PolishedDocs/` candidate. Report the exact blocker and stop after reading only what is needed to establish it.

Safe expression work may continue only when it is genuinely independent of the blocked core and the Human explicitly authorized a scoped excerpt or isolated evaluation. Put an evaluation artifact outside the repository, label it non-candidate, and do not let it imply that the full chapter is ready. Never silently solve the blocked item in `PolishedDocs/`.

## 3. Material-role Tests

### Author project

Use `longitudinal case` as the role for the author's ongoing projects. State whether an episode generates a question, constrains a theory, or exposes a boundary. Use it to show chronology, constraint, failure, and judgment change. Identify the observed version or period when it matters. Preserve unresolved status and plausible alternative explanations. Do not infer industry validity from recurrence inside the same project family.

### Design Hypothesis

A useful Design Hypothesis answers four questions:

1. What must be true for the design to make sense?
2. Through what mechanism is it expected to help?
3. Under what condition would it fail or become unnecessary?
4. What future observation would keep, narrow, or withdraw it?

If the source cannot answer all four, state the missing part naturally or route the hypothesis for further design work. Do not manufacture certainty to make the paragraph flow.

### External case

Prefer a primary artifact, official engineering record, project documentation, paper, policy, or postmortem. Record publication date or version when stability matters.

An external case earns space only if removing it would weaken a comparison, counterexample, transfer test, or historical precedent. A second example saying the same thing in different branding is not corroboration by itself.

Before integration, ask:

- Is the source reporting its own system, measuring an effect, proposing guidance, or interpreting history?
- Does the case share the relevant mechanism, or only similar vocabulary?
- What important difference limits transfer to Beyond Code?
- Is the chapter using the case as evidence, illustration, or design stimulus?
- If removing the case name loses no case-specific constraint, is the case only decorative? When case facts remain, keep their source attribution.

External case selection, a Source Map, and a comparison label are not Evidence Receipts. This Skill does not perform research or formal Evidence work. Pause and return to the main task when the proposed prose depends on precise history or runtime behavior, asserts an effect, generalizes across projects, states a high-risk fact, reports conflicting material, or would directly change AI Game Dev OS behavior. Resume only after the separate workflow updates the accepted baseline.

An editorial candidate map such as [W0 RR-040](../../../../RevisedResult/RR-040_公开案例组合与章节映射.md) may identify a future comparison, but it does not by itself authorize the case facts to enter prose. Use the case only after Human adoption and the required logic／Evidence routing are visible in the accepted baseline.

### Textbook facility

Use definitions, diagrams, summaries, exercises, or Field Guides when they help the reader navigate or act. Do not add them merely to make chapters symmetrical. Full learning objectives and exercises remain optional until a teaching context is defined.

## 4. Drafting Decisions

Prefer decisions that restore causality and judgment over surface variation:

- move a definition after the problem that makes it necessary;
- replace repeated taxonomies with one worked conflict;
- let a counterexample interrupt an overly clean argument;
- keep an unresolved question open instead of ending every section with a principle;
- compress repeated Schema explanations while retaining Canonical model precision, the owner expression, and necessary consumer Bridges;
- vary section shape because the material differs, not because a detector rewards variation.

Do not add fabricated anecdotes, decorative quotations, fake hesitation, or casual asides.

## 5. Six Fidelity Checks

### Source Fidelity

- Every retained or added factual detail has the right source.
- Author experience is traceable and chronology is not reconstructed beyond the record.
- Compression preserves counterevidence and material limitations.

### Epistemic Fidelity

- Observable fact, contemporaneous judgment, retrospective inference, and current recommendation remain distinguishable in natural language.
- The prose does not turn absence of evidence into evidence of absence.

### Model Fidelity

- Terms retain their owner, type, and cross-chapter relation.
- Authority, Evidence, Decision, Adoption, and Runtime state are not collapsed for fluency.
- A consuming chapter does not silently redefine a Canonical object.

### Hypothesis Fidelity

- Design assumptions remain conditional.
- Premise, expected mechanism, failure condition, and change signal remain visible where material.
- A proposed design is not presented as implemented or effective without appropriate evidence.

### Case Role / Provenance Fidelity

- Each external case has one primary useful role, any secondary role adds a distinct constraint, and all roles have a visible support limit.
- First-party reports establish what a team reported or recorded; underlying behavior, causal effects, and general validity require matching support.
- One project or vendor case is not used to prove cross-project, organizational, or industry effects.
- Shared Provenance and relevant version or time boundaries remain intact.

### Reader Fidelity

- A practitioner can identify the problem before carrying the terminology load.
- Macro structure is navigable; micro-structure does not repeat mechanically.
- Technical tools earn their space.
- The chapter makes clear what the author observed, inferred, recommends, and still does not know.

## 6. Human Review Hand-off

Return the candidate with a compact note covering:

- the chosen narrative center;
- the largest structural changes;
- material retained despite compression;
- substantive issues routed out of the prose pass;
- questions that require the author's experience, aesthetics, or final judgment.

Do not label the candidate accepted, final, or publication-ready before the Human decides. Acceptance of voice alone does not decide logic, Evidence, or publication status.
